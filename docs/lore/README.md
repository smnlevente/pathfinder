## In one paragraph

Earth is failing. A small team of scientists has built the **Pathfinder**, a machine that opens stable wormholes, and
sends pilots through it, one ship at a time, to cross three uncharted galaxies and find a world humanity can live on.
Most ships don't come back. Their **Black Boxes** do: the Pathfinder pulls each one home through the wormhole, and with
it the data and exotic matter the pilot gathered, so the next ship flies better armed and better informed. Somewhere
past the third galaxy is **New Home**.
