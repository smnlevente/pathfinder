# The galaxies

[Docs home](../README.md) › [Lore](README.md) › The galaxies

A run crosses three galaxies. Each one is further from home and stranger than the last, and each is held by a
guardian.

## 1. The Shattered Expanse

Asteroid fields, drifting rock, and the gutted hulls of an old mining operation: freighters stripped to the frame,
cargo bays hanging open, security grids that still hum. Whoever worked these fields left in a hurry, and left their
defences running.

The Remnant got here first. Their crimson machines patrol the debris, and their toll gates block the jump lanes. Rich
in salvage, it is where every pilot learns to fly.

**Guardian: the Quarry Maw.** A colossal rock-worm that tunnels through the asteroid debris, armoured in stone. The
miners who fled may have been fleeing it. It burrows under its prey, erupts in a storm of rock, and charges through
whole fields of debris. Only the yellow crystals growing along its body, and the soft maw it opens to feed, can be
hurt.

## 2. The Graveyard Drift

Derelict stations, debris fields and the defence grids of a civilisation that died long before humanity looked up.
Nobody knows what killed them. Their machines didn't notice: weapons platforms still track anything that moves, and
the dead stations still guard their doors.

The Remnant have learned to strip and refit what they find here: flamethrowers, arc emitters and heavy crushers built
on alien frames.

**Guardian: the Binary Wardens.** The defence intelligence of the largest station, split across two cores so that
neither could ever be destroyed alone. Each keeps the other alive: bring one down, and its twin reboots it within
seconds. Joined by a tether of raw current, the two circle their dead station endlessly. Only a pilot who breaks both at
once gets through.

## 3. The Veil Nebula

A living nebula, glowing from within. Bioluminescent hives drift in its clouds, spores settle on passing hulls and
grow there, and the space itself seems to breathe. This is the home of **the Swarm**.

**Guardian: the Hive Matriarch**. An alien queen half-fused with the nebula itself. She births the Swarm, fills the
clouds with spores that choke an engine and eat a shield, and when cornered she blooms: the whole nebula pulses outward
from her in rings of living light. Her final cry is the last thing between humanity and its new home.

> [!NOTE]
> The Veil Nebula's own look and Swarm enemy roster are still to come; for now its levels use the shared arena and
> enemy mix, and its guardian is the Matriarch.

## New Home

Past the Veil Nebula, the long-range scans resolve into a world: water, air, a sun that holds steady. Reaching it wins
the run. The first time, the ending plays; the Pathfinder locks the route, and humanity finally has somewhere to go.

## Later: theme pools

The GDD plans for each galaxy tier to draw from a pool of themes, so tier 2 might be the Graveyard Drift in one run
and an Ice Moon system in the next. Each theme would bring its own hazards (drifting asteroids, electrified debris and
laser grids, spore clouds), its own enemy skins and its own arena layouts.

## Open questions

- What killed the civilisation of the Graveyard Drift? The Swarm is the obvious suspect; it would tie the second and
  third galaxies together.
- Do the miners of the Shattered Expanse have a story? Were they an earlier human expedition, a Remnant outpost, or
  someone else entirely?
- Is New Home inhabited?
