# Factions & bestiary

[Docs home](../README.md) › [Lore](README.md) › Factions & bestiary

## The Remnant

A rival human splinter group. When the Pathfinder project was young, they stole its early wormhole technology and
went through on their own. Now they scavenge the same galaxies the pilots cross, and treat every jump lane as their
territory.

- **Style:** precise, geometric, mechanical. Their machines are crimson, their bullet patterns are rings and fans with
  exact gaps, and every attack is announced by the same red-orange flare.
- **Methods:** automated fleets rather than crews, lures disguised as old Pathfinder distress beacons, and toll gates
  across the jump lanes ("*This sector is ours, Pathfinder. Pay the toll, or turn around.*").
- **What they want:** the same thing humanity wants, a new world, but only for themselves.

*In the game:* most of the enemies you fight are Remnant machines; they spring the ambushes behind fake distress calls
and run the toll gates in Mystery events.

## The Swarm

Hostile alien organisms native to the Veil Nebula. Their attacks are fluid and erratic, with organic bullet patterns
that flow rather than tick, and their bodies glow a toxic green. Where the Remnant are clockwork, the Swarm is weather.
So far the Matriarch and her Broodlings are built; the rest of the Swarm is planned for galaxy 3.

## The machines

The enemies as a pilot's field notes might describe them. For the numbers, see [Enemies](../game/enemies.md).

| Machine | Field notes |
|---|---|
| **Drone** | A Remnant interceptor: a spinning blade with a cheap brain that rams. Never alone. |
| **Gunner** | A turret on a hover base that keeps its distance and fires short, aimed bursts. Its eye glows before it shoots. |
| **Spinner** | A slow emitter disc that sprays rings of shot, turning a little each time. Built to deny space, not to chase. |
| **Lancer** | An arrowhead built for one thing: ramming at full burn. It shows its line before it goes, and skids for a long time after. |
| **Carrier** | A Remnant drone tender: an armoured bow, launch bays down its flanks, and an exposed reactor at the stern. |
| **Scorcher** | A salvaged mining cutter turned flamethrower, its fuel drum strapped to its back. Short reach, terrible at it. |
| **Arc Drone** | A Graveyard Drift sentry the Remnant rewired. Its coils lock onto a target and discharge along a single line. |
| **Crusher** | A heavy demolition frame on treads, too massive to push around. It only needs to get close once. |

## The guardians

Each galaxy's heart is held by something that doesn't belong to either faction. Guardians are why a galaxy can't
simply be flown through: the Pathfinder can only open the next wormhole once its guardian falls, and the Singularity
Core at its centre is the most valuable thing a pilot can send home.

| Guardian | Galaxy | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **The Quarry Maw** | The Shattered Expanse | A colossal rock-worm, armoured in stone, with crystal growths along its body |
| **The Binary Wardens** | The Graveyard Drift | A dead civilisation's station defence AI, split across two cores that reboot each other |
| **The Hive Matriarch** | The Veil Nebula | The Swarm's queen, half-fused with the nebula; her brood dies with her |

## Others out there

Not everyone in the void is hostile. The Mystery events hint at a wider cast:

- **The Wandering Engineer**, who patches hulls and tunes equipment for the right price.
- **Scrap traders**, whose barges bristle with salvage and who'll swap tech or buy yours.
- **Lost Pathfinder scouts**, whose escape pods still drift with their cargo sealed.

## Open questions

- Is "the Remnant" the final name, and what is their story with the Pathfinder team? (A former team member? A
  government that abandoned the project?)
- Are the Shattered Expanse machines Remnant-built, or older miner defences the Remnant took over?
- Do the Remnant and the Swarm fight each other? A three-way battle would make a strong Mystery event or level.
